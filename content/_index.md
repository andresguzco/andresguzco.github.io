I am a PhD student in computer science at [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en), supervised by [Kirill Neklyudov](https://necludov.github.io). My expertise is in machine learning and deep learning; the problems I apply it to come from physics and chemistry. My current focus is how to scale ab initio methods like density functional theory to massive systems.

Before Mila I did an MPhil at the [University of Amsterdam](https://www.uva.nl) with [Jan-Willem van de Meent](https://jwvdm.github.io) on variational inference in flow-based generative models, and wrote my thesis at the [University of Toronto](https://web.cs.toronto.edu) with [Alán Aspuru-Guzik](https://www.matter.toronto.edu/basic-content-page/about-alan) on Bayesian and manifold optimization. My BSc was at the [Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam](https://www.vu.nl) with [Andre Lucas](https://sites.google.com/view/alucas), on statistical models for particle tracking.

Outside of research I climb, hike and camp.
